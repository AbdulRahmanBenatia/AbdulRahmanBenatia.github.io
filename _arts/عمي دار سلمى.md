    <div class="poem">

      <!-- Bayt 1 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          عمي دارَ سلمى واسلمي طيلةَ الدهرِ<br>
          وقفتُ بها أبكي وقد أنفدتْ صبْري
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Good morrow, abode of Salma, and be safe for all time! I stood there weeping, my patience spent by
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 2 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          حوادثُ لو مسّتْ من القومِ راهبًا<br>
          تقيًّا أحالتهُ إلى شدةِ الكفرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          misfortunes that, had they touched the most devout monk among the people, would have turned him to the depths of unbelief.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 3 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          فلم ألقَ لي فيها ملاذًا يضمُّني<br>
          ولا من أهاليها الأحبةِ من ذكْرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          I found no refuge there to hold me, and no trace of its beloved people.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 4 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          فساءلتُها عن أهلِها خلةً مضتْ<br>
          وهل ردَّ سآلًا قُبيليَ  من جدْرِ!
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          I asked it about its people, friends now gone. But has a wall ever answered anyone who asked before me?
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 5 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          فسحّتْ دموعي، كلما رمتُ ردّها<br>
          تشكَّتْ -وزادتْ- من تعذُّرِ ذا الأمرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          My tears poured down; whenever I tried to hold them back, they complained, and flowed the more, that this could not be done.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 6 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          تذكّرتُ والذكرى حياةٌ وميتةٌ<br>
          نعيمًا بخلٍّ غابَ من قلةِ الشكرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          I remembered, and memory is both a life and a death, the bliss I knew with a friend who went away for want of thanks:
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 7 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          جوادٍ كأن البحرَ بعضُ عطائهِ<br>
          حليمٍ على الجهالِ يلقونَ بالجمرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          so generous the sea seems only part of his giving, so forbearing with the ignorant who pelt him with burning coals,
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 8 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          قديرٍ على الأعداءِ إن سَلَّ سيفَهُ<br>
           ترى القومَ في سعدٍ يغنونَ بالنصرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          so mighty over his enemies that when he draws his sword, you see his people rejoicing, singing of victory,
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 9 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          أديبٍ رضيناهُ أميرًا على الورى<br>
          بهيٍّ كأنْ قد مدَّ بالنور للبدرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          a man of letters we accepted as prince over all people, so radiant he might have lent the full moon its light,
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 10 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          حكيمٍ بما يختارُ ما اختارَ بينما<br>
          أعاتبُه أن راح في خيرةٍ هجري
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          wise in all he chooses, whatever he has chosen, even as I reproach him for choosing to abandon me.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 11 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          فلا تعرضنْ عني فما ليَ ملجأٌ<br>
          إليهِ استنادي في المياسرِ والعسرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          So do not turn away from me, for I have no refuge to lean on, in times of ease or hardship,
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 12 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          سواكَ وإني أرتضي اللومَ منكمُ<br>
          ولكنَّما بالهجرِ أدخلتَني قبري
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          but you. I gladly accept your blame, but by abandoning me you have laid me in my grave.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 13 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          إذا ما عليَّ الدهرُ ألقى سهامَهُ<br>
          تلقّيتُها سمحًا معَ الحلمِ والصبرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          When time hurls its arrows at me, I meet them with an easy heart, with forbearance and patience.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 14 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          ولكنْ دهتني منكَ يا صاحِ ضربةٌ<br>
          فلا صبرَ أقواهُ ولا سلوَ بالخمرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          But a blow from you, my friend, has struck me; I have no patience strong enough for it, and wine brings no forgetting.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 15 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          هجرتَ الذي يرضاكَ في الحلمِ والجفا<br>
          وعاهدَ قبلَ الطيْبِ منكَ على المرِّ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          You abandoned the one who accepts you in gentleness and in harshness, who pledged himself to your bitterness before your sweetness.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 16 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          سوادٌ كسا دهري لبعدِكَ، عالمي<br>
          توفّيَ فيه النبتُ من غيبةِ القطرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Blackness has clothed my days since you left; my world is a place where the plants have died for want of rain.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 17 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          وحاشا أردّ اليومَ حكمَكَ، إنما<br>
          أقرُّ بما أخطأتُ والحزنُ في صدري
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Far be it from me to reject your judgement today; I only confess my wrong, with grief in my chest.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 18 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          فلا تحملنْ عليَّ ما لا أطيقُه<br>
          وعاتبْ بما تهواهُ لكن بلا هجرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          So do not lay on me more than I can bear; reproach me however you wish, but do not abandon me.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 19 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          لقدْ طالَ منكَ النأيُ عني فكفّهُ<br>
          أعيذُكَ من ردٍّ الذي جاءَ بالعذرِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Your distance from me has lasted too long, so end it. God protect you from turning away one who comes with an apology.
        </div>
      </div>

    </div>
