      <!-- Introduction -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          كان صديقي يمتدحُ كلَّ تعبيرٍ صادقٍ كتبتُه من تجربةٍ مررتُ بها، أرجو أن يكون قد أحب ما عبّرتُ به عنه حين قلتُ:
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          My friend used to praise every honest expression I wrote out of something I had lived through. I hope he liked what I expressed about him when I said:
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 1 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          صحبتُكَ عندَ سرّاءٍ وضرٍّ<br>
          وكنتَ المصطفى بينَ الرفاقِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          I stood by you in joy and in hardship, and you were the chosen one among my companions.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 2 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          نسيتَ عهودَنا وصرمتَ حبلي<br>
          وأسلمتَ المودةَ للشقاقِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          You forgot our promises, cut the bond between us, and surrendered our affection to discord.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 3 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          فآثرتُ الفراقَ وكنتُ قبلًا<br>
          أظنُّ الموتَ أقربَ مِن فراقِ!
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          So I chose parting, though I once believed that death would come sooner than parting!
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Closing -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          برّح بي الشوقُ واللهِ!
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          By God, longing has worn me out!
        </div>
      </div>

    </div>
