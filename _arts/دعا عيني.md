<div class="poem-page">
  <div class="poem-container">
    <button id="toggle-translation">Show translation</button>

    <div class="poem">
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">دعا عيني -بربِّكما- تفيضُ</span>
        <span class="original">وإن أنا قلتُ، ما يغني القريضُ!</span>
        <span class="translation">My eyes, by your Lord, well up with tears,  
And if I speak, words of poetry cannot suffice!</span>
      </div>

      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">مضى عامٌ ولم أرَ بعدُ قومي</span>
        <span class="original">وكان البعدُ في عظمي يهيضُ</span>
        <span class="translation">A year has passed, yet I have not seen my people,  
And the distance gnaws deep within my bones.</span>
      </div>

      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">فهل لي والزمانُ يمرُّ حظٌّ؟</span>
        <span class="original">وهل يبدو مع العامِ الوميضُ؟</span>
        <span class="translation">Is there any fortune for me as time passes?  
Will the year bring even a faint gleam?</span>
      </div>

      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">وميضُ لقائنا يشفي صدورًا</span>
        <span class="original">ويُسعدُ من له جدٌّ حضيضُ</span>
        <span class="translation">The spark of our meeting heals hearts,  
And brings joy even to those whose hope is low.</span>
      </div>

      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">فإن يجُدِ الزمان لنا بهذا</span>
        <span class="original">فعن عيبِ الزمانِ أنا غضيضُ</span>
        <span class="translation">If time grants us this blessing,  
Then I am freed from the faults of the ages.</span>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>



<script>
document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', () => {
  const button = document.getElementById('toggle-translation');
  const translations = document.querySelectorAll('.poem .translation');

  translations.forEach(t => t.style.display = 'none'); // hide initially

  button.addEventListener('click', () => {
    const isVisible = translations[0].style.display === 'inline';
    translations.forEach(t => t.style.display = isVisible ? 'none' : 'inline');
    button.textContent = isVisible ? 'Show translation' : 'Hide translation';
  });
});
</script>
