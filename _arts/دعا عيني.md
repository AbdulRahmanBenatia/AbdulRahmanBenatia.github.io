    <div class="poem">
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">دعا عيني -بربِّكما- تفيضُ</span>
        <span class="original">وإن أنا قلتُ، ما يغني القريضُ!</span>
        <span class="translation">Let my eyes, by your Lord, well up with tears,  
And if I speak, words of poetry cannot suffice!</span>
      </div>

      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">مضى عامٌ ولم أرَ بعدُ قومي</span>
        <span class="original">وكان البعدُ في عظمي يهيضُ</span>
        <span class="translation">A year has passed, yet I have not seen my people,  
And the distance gnaws deep within my bones.</span>
      </div>

      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">فهل لي والزمانُ يمرُّ حظٌّ؟</span>
        <span class="original">وهل يبدو مع العامِ الوميضُ؟</span>
        <span class="translation">Is there any fortune for me as time passes?  
Will the year bring even a faint gleam?</span>
      </div>

      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">وميضُ لقائنا يشفي صدورًا</span>
        <span class="original">ويُسعدُ من له جدٌّ حضيضُ</span>
        <span class="translation">The spark of our meeting healing hearts,  
And bringing joy even to those whose hope is low.</span>
      </div>

      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">فإن يجُدِ الزمان لنا بهذا</span>
        <span class="original">فعن عيبِ الزمانِ أنا غضيضُ</span>
        <span class="translation">If time grants us this blessing,  
Then I will turn my gaze away from the faults the time made.</span>
      </div>
