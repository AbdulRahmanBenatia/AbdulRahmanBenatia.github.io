      <!-- Bayt 1 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          ولما أن بدت ليلى<br>
          أخذتُ أســبحُ المولى
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          And when Layla (symbol) appeared,  
          I began to glorify the Lord.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 2 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          تجلّى نورها يجلو<br>
          ظلام الليل واستولى
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          Her light shone forth, clearing away  
          the darkness of the night.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 3 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          على قلبي فهيمني<br>
          فلســت أرى لذا حلّا
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          It overwhelmed my heart and left me bewildered,  
          so that I see no solution.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 4 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          ورمتُ بأن أكلمها<br>
          فلم أسـطعْ لها قولا
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          I longed to speak to her,  
          yet I could not utter a word.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 5 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          أيا ليلى أيا ليلى<br>
          سلبتِ القلبَ والعقلا
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          O Layla, O Layla,  
          you have stolen both my heart and reason.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 6 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          أنا عبدٌ لكم يرجو<br>
          وصالًا فاسمحوا طولا
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          I am your humble servant, hoping  
          for union; so grant me your grace.
        </span>
      </div>

    </div>
