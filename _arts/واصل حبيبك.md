    <p> These lines were written just after my grandfather passed away in 2023. <\p>


    <div class="poem">

      <!-- Bayt 1 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          واصِلْ حبيبَك لا تقطعْ ولا تُبْدِ<br>
          له الجفاءَ ولا تركنْ إلى البُعْدِ
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          Stay connected to your beloved—do not sever ties nor show  
          coldness, and do not incline toward distance.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 2 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          وكن على سعةٍ إن جاء عن خطأٍ<br>
          ما لست ترضاه (أو إن كان عن عمْدِ)
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          Be forgiving if something comes from them in error—  
          that you do not accept, or even if intentional.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 3 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          واعلمْ بأنك يومًا ما مفارقُه<br>
          ما أهلَك الناسَ في الأيامِ كالفقدِ!
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          And know that one day you will apart from them—  
          nothing has destroyed people through time like loss!
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 4 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
            فيا خسارةَ نفسي يا ندامتَها<br>
            إن قيل ماتَ وآخرُ عهدِه صدِّي 💔
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
            What a loss for my soul, what regret—  
            if it is said: “He died,” and the last thing he saw from me was my turning away. 💔
        </span>
        </div>

    </div>
  </div>
