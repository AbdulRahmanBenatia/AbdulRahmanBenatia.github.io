      <!-- Bayt 1 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          جاؤوا لكلِّهِمُ يا ربِّ مسألةٌ<br>
          وجئتُ ما لي سوى الرضوانِ من سؤْلِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Others came to You, O Lord, with their requests,  
          while I came with nothing but seeking Your pleasure.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 2 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          حقِّقْ به أملي<br>
          حسِّنْ به عملي<br>
          اغفرْ به زللي<br>
          في القولِ والفعْلِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Fulfill through it my hope,  
          improve through it my deeds,  
          forgive through it my slips,  
          in speech and action.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 3 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          يا ربِّ يا سندي<br>
          ها قد مددتُ يدي<br>
          أرجو ومعتمَدي<br>
          بالعفوِ والفضْلِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          My Lord, my support,  
          I have stretched out my hands,  
          with hope, and my reliance  
          is upon Your pardon and grace.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 4 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          قد جئتُ معترفًا<br>
          بالذنبِ مقترفًا<br>
          أن كنتُ معتكفًا<br>
          في اللهوِ والهزْلِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          I have come confessing,  
          bearing my sins,  
          that I had been immersed  
          in distraction and play.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 5 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          لكنَّ لي قلبًا<br>
          ملّأتَه حبًّا<br>
          أزلْ له حجْبًا<br>
          واسمح بذا الوصْلِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Yet I have a heart  
          filled by You with love,  
          remove its veils  
          and grant it union [with You].
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 6 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          واجبرْه في كرمٍ<br>
          إذ جاءَ في ألمٍ<br>
          ظمآنَ في ظُلَمٍ<br>
          بالنورِ والعَلِّ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Heal it with Your generosity,  
          as it comes in pain 
          thirsty in darkness,  
          with Your light and quenching of thirst.
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 7 -->
      <div class="line">
        <div class="original">
          ثم الصلاةُ على<br>
          مَنْ قد علا وعلا<br>
          وعلى الصحابِ الُالَى<br>
          والَوه والأهلِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
          Then blessings upon  
          the one who rose in rank and honor,  
          and upon the noble companions,  
          his followers, and his family.
        </div>
      </div>
