---
title: "Evening in Distress"
categories: poetry
type: poetry
date: 2026-02-06
---
<div class="poem-page">
  <div class="poem-container">
    <button id="toggle-translation">Show translation</button>

    <div class="poem">
      <!-- Bayt 1 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          فإن أمسيتُ في كربٍ فكم ذا<br>
          قطعتُ البيدَ في طلبِ العوالي
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          If I found myself in distress this evening, then many a time I crossed the desert in pursuit of lofty goals!
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 2 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          وهوّنتُ الخطوبَ -وقد توالتْ-<br>
          وجاوزتُ الأواخرَ والأوالي
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          And I lightened the hardships as they came, and surpassed both the last and the first of men.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 3 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          وكنتُ لمَن أرادَ العونَ، حتى<br>
          رددتُ البأسَ عن صحبٍ وآلِ
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          And I was the aid for those who sought help, turning back harm from friends and family.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 4 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          ولم أمددْ يدَ المحتاجِ يومًا<br>
          فإن تُمدد يداي فللمعالي!
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          And I never extended my hand to the needy in vain; if my hands are extended, it is for noble pursuits alone.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 5 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          أنا الفجرُ الذي جلّى الليالي<br>
          فما لي اليوم جلَّ رديءُ حالي!
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          I am the dawn that clears the night, then wht my condition today seems so bleak!
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 6 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          وما ليَ والهوى! قد كنتُ قبلًا<br>
          خليًّا لا عليَّ بهِ ولا لي
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          What has love to do with me? I once was solo, burdenless, belonging to no one.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 7 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          وما ليَ والنوى! والبعدُ أضنى<br>
          وأقتلُ للشجاعِ من النبالِ
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          What has distance to do with me? Separation wears me down, more deadly than any arrow to the brave.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 8 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          وما ليَ والجوى! يزدادُ شوقي<br>
          ولا يُدرَى سبيلٌ للوصالِ
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          What has longing to do with me? My yearning grows, yet the path to union remains unknown.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 9 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          فلو أني استطعتُ سلوتُ، لكنْ<br>
          سلوّي عنه ضربٌ من مُحالِ!
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          If I could forget, I would.. but forgetting them is kind of impossible.
        </span>
      </div>

      <!-- Bayt 10 -->
      <div class="line">
        <span class="original">
          "وأفجعُ مَن فقدْنا مَن وجدْنا<br>
          قبيلَ الفقدِ مفقودَ المثالِ"
        </span>
        <span class="translation">
          The most bitter loss is losing someone, when even before their absence we knew no one else could compare.
        </span>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

    const isVisible = translations[0].style.display === 'inline';
    translations.forEach(t => t.style.display = isVisible ? 'none' : 'inline');
    button.textContent = isVisible ? 'Show translation' : 'Hide translation';
  });
});
</script>
